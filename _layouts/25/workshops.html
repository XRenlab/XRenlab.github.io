Call for Workshop / Course Proposals

We invite proposals for in‑person workshops / courses that explore contemporary topics in Human–Computer Interaction. These will take place on Friday, November 21, 2025, the opening day of the conference in Singapore.


🧭 Scope & Topics

Proposals should address cutting-edge areas within HCI, including but not limited to:

  • Emerging interaction paradigms & UX design
  • Human-AI collaboration, fairness, agency, and interpretability
  • UX and social computing, digital wellbeing, and ethics
  • AR/VR/XR, ubiquitous computing, and embodied interaction
  • HCI for sustainability, accessibility, inclusion
  • Novel methodologies in HCI research and evaluation
  • Application domains: health, education, civic tech, workplace tech

📄 Submission Guidelines

Proposals (up to 3 pages, excluding references) should follow this proposal template.

🧩 What We Offer

  • A dedicated half (3.5 hr) or full-day (2 x 3.5 hr) slot during the conference program
  • Standard room setup with projector, mic, Wi‑Fi
  • Conference-promoted workshop page and inclusion in proceedings
  • Strong local and regional HCI community attendance

📌 Audience Participation & Fees

  • Workshop organisers and participants must register for both the workshop and the main conference
  • No separate workshop fee; access included with conference registration.
  • Minimum attendance of 10 in-person participants required
